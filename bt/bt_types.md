# Survey Report on Types of Blockchains and Their Real-Time Use Cases

## 1. Introduction

Blockchain is a distributed digital ledger technology used to store information securely and transparently. It records transactions in blocks that are connected using cryptographic techniques. Once information is added to the blockchain, it is difficult to modify or delete.

Blockchain technology is widely used in banking, healthcare, education, supply chain management, government services, and digital identity systems.

## 2. Objectives

- To understand the concept of blockchain technology.
- To study different types of blockchains.
- To explore real-time applications of blockchain.
- To compare the advantages and limitations of different blockchain types.

## 3. Types of Blockchains

There are four major types of blockchains:

### 3.1 Public Blockchain

A public blockchain is an open and decentralized network where anyone can join, read transactions, and participate in the network.

**Examples:**
- Bitcoin
- Ethereum
- Solana

**Real-Time Use Cases:**
- Cryptocurrency transactions.
- Decentralized Finance (DeFi).
- NFT marketplaces.
- Smart contract applications.
- International digital payments.

### 3.2 Private Blockchain

A private blockchain is a restricted network controlled by a single organization. Only authorized users can access and participate in the network.

**Examples:**
- Hyperledger Fabric
- MultiChain

**Real-Time Use Cases:**
- Banking and financial records.
- Healthcare data management.
- Employee information management.
- Secure document sharing.
- Internal business transactions.

### 3.3 Consortium Blockchain

A consortium blockchain is managed by multiple organizations instead of a single organization. Only selected organizations can participate in the network.

**Examples:**
- R3 Corda
- Hyperledger Fabric consortium networks

**Real-Time Use Cases:**
- Supply chain management.
- Interbank transactions.
- Trade finance.
- Insurance claim processing.
- Logistics management.

### 3.4 Hybrid Blockchain

A hybrid blockchain combines the features of public and private blockchains. It allows organizations to keep sensitive information private while making selected information publicly accessible.

**Examples:**
- XinFin (XDC Network)
- Dragonchain

**Real-Time Use Cases:**
- Government record management.
- Healthcare systems.
- Real estate transactions.
- Supply chain tracking.
- Digital identity management.

## 4. Real-Time Applications of Blockchain

### 4.1 Banking and Finance

Blockchain enables secure and faster financial transactions. It reduces the need for intermediaries and improves transaction transparency.

### 4.2 Healthcare

Blockchain helps securely store and share patient medical records. It allows authorized healthcare professionals to access reliable patient information.

### 4.3 Supply Chain Management

Blockchain tracks products from manufacturers to customers. It improves transparency and helps identify counterfeit products.

**Example:** Walmart has used blockchain technology to improve food traceability.

### 4.4 Education

Blockchain can store academic certificates, marksheets, and student records securely. It also helps institutions verify certificates and reduce fake qualifications.

### 4.5 Government Services

Blockchain can be used for maintaining land records, digital identity, certificates, and public documents. It improves transparency and reduces unauthorized modifications.

### 4.6 Real Estate

Blockchain can maintain property ownership records and support transparent property transactions. It can reduce paperwork and simplify verification.

### 4.7 Digital Identity

Blockchain can provide secure digital identity systems. Users can control and share verified identity information with authorized organizations.

### 4.8 Insurance

Blockchain can automate insurance claim processing through smart contracts. It helps reduce paperwork, delays, and fraudulent claims.

## 5. Comparison of Blockchain Types

| Feature | Public | Private | Consortium | Hybrid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Access | Open to everyone | Restricted | Selected organizations | Mixed access |
| Control | Decentralized | Single organization | Multiple organizations | Combined |
| Transparency | High | Limited | Shared among members | Configurable |
| Speed | Generally slower | Generally faster | Generally faster | Depends on design |
| Security | Cryptographic and consensus-based | Access-controlled | Shared control | Combined security |
| Example | Ethereum | Hyperledger Fabric | R3 Corda | XDC Network |

## 6. Advantages of Blockchain

- **Transparency:** Transactions can be tracked and verified.
- **Security:** Cryptographic techniques protect stored information.
- **Decentralization:** Reduces dependence on a central authority.
- **Data Integrity:** Makes unauthorized modification difficult.
- **Traceability:** Helps track transactions and products.
- **Automation:** Smart contracts can execute predefined operations automatically.

## 7. Limitations of Blockchain

- High energy consumption in some consensus mechanisms.
- Scalability and transaction-speed challenges.
- Implementation and maintenance costs.
- Regulatory and legal uncertainties.
- Difficulty in modifying incorrect recorded information.
- Privacy concerns when sensitive information is stored improperly.

## 8. Survey Findings

The survey shows that blockchain technology is useful in different industries. Public blockchains are commonly used for cryptocurrencies and decentralized applications. Private blockchains are suitable for organizations that require restricted access. Consortium blockchains support collaboration between multiple organizations, while hybrid blockchains provide a combination of public transparency and private data management.

Blockchain has significant potential in finance, healthcare, education, supply chains, and government services.

## 9. Future Scope

- Wider adoption of blockchain in banking and finance.
- Integration with Artificial Intelligence and the Internet of Things.
- Improved scalability and transaction speed.
- Development of secure digital identity systems.
- Increased use of smart contracts.
- Better interoperability between different blockchain networks.

## 10. Conclusion

Blockchain is an emerging technology that provides secure, transparent, and decentralized data management. The four major types of blockchain are Public, Private, Consortium, and Hybrid. Each type has different features and applications depending on organizational requirements.

Blockchain is transforming industries such as banking, healthcare, education, supply chain management, and government services. With improvements in scalability, security, and interoperability, blockchain technology is expected to play an important role in future digital systems.
